Counts are of unique reports, as CauseTable counts them. The export pages overlap, so some reports appear twice in reports-4471.json; each id counts once. A one-day report stays counted under its tenant's cause, as the desk files it, and also has its own row.

How each report was placed

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first, and every tenant on file writes the day first. A booking for 4 March comes out as 3 April. Reports by app and email, and by form from booking-v2 tenants.
  • hint: booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at day-first tenants (Alder Physio, Copper Studio, Pale Harbour) type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant's way. The result looks like the parser bug, but the parser fix does not touch it: the hint has to change.
  • one-day: the date moves by one day and the month stays right ("Tuesday 12 May, calendar says Monday 11 May"; Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday). That is not a swapped month, so it is a time-zone or date-boundary bug. The desk already listed these in desk-notes.json, and CauseTable counts them under parser with a footnote. They need their own fix.

Why #4471 looked like one problem

  • The desk merges on subject line alone (decision 0001). All three problems arrive as "Date is wrong" or "Booking on the wrong date".
  • Intake's tag is a guess from the reporter's browser locale, not from the tenant. The "intake tag agrees" column shows how often it is right.
  • CauseTable groups by tenant and channel only, so a one-day move lands under parser.

What to do

  1. Keep the parser fix on #4471's queue: it covers the parser row.
  2. Change booking-v3's hint to the tenant's own date order, or make the form follow its hint.
  3. Look for the one-day move in time-zone handling: where an invite turns a local date into a time and back.
  4. When you merge a date report, read the body and place it with the tenant's locale and form, not with its subject or intake tag.
